02 / OUR VISION

Our
vision.

“Understand the environment. Act for its future.”

The Environmental Sciences chapter invites students to look beyond the surface and understand the living systems that shape our world. From ecosystems and biodiversity to climate, pollution and conservation, we explore the connections between environmental change and everyday life. Through learning, observation, research and practical action, we aim to turn environmental awareness into informed choices and inspire students to become thoughtful stewards of the world around them.

03 / A MESSAGE FROM THE CHAPTER

A warm
welcome.

Chapter Coordinator

I am Dr. Anum Tariq, Chapter Coordinator for Environmental Sciences, and I am delighted to welcome you to our chapter. Environmental Sciences is not only about understanding what is happening to our planet, but also about asking why it is happening and what we can do about it. Here, I encourage you to stay curious, question what you see around you and look at environmental challenges from more than one perspective. Whether your interest lies in climate, biodiversity, ecosystems, pollution or conservation, there is always something new to discover. I look forward to seeing you learn, explore and turn your understanding of the environment into meaningful action.
