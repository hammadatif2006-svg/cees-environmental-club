02 / OUR VISION

Our
vision.

“Travel responsibly. Value places. Protect experiences.”

The Tourism & Hospitality Management chapter invites students to look beyond the journey itself and understand the people, places and environments that make every experience possible. We explore responsible tourism, sustainable hospitality, destinations and community connections while asking how the way we travel can shape the places we visit. Through learning, creativity and practical engagement, we aim to inspire a new generation of travellers and hospitality professionals who value places, respect communities and leave a positive footprint.

03 / A MESSAGE FROM THE CHAPTER

A warm
welcome.

Chapter Coordinator

I’m Muhammad Dastgeer, your Chapter Coordinator for Tourism & Hospitality Management, and I’m pleased to welcome you to our chapter. I want you to see tourism as more than just travelling and hospitality as more than simply serving people. It is about understanding places, respecting cultures, connecting with communities and recognising the impact every journey can leave behind. This chapter is a space for you to explore, question, create and discover new ways of making tourism more responsible and sustainable. Whether you dream of exploring the world, building meaningful experiences or simply understanding it better, there is a place for you here. So come along, stay curious and let’s make every journey count.
