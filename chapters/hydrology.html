02 / OUR VISION

Our
vision.

“Understanding water. Protecting what sustains us.”

Water connects everything. The HWRM chapter brings students together to explore the systems that move it, the resources that depend on it and the challenges that threaten its future. Through science, field learning, discussion and action, we want students to see water differently, understand its value and become part of protecting what sustains us.

03 / A MESSAGE FROM THE CHAPTER

A warm
welcome.

Chapter Coordinator

I am Dr. Muhammad Waqar, Chapter Coordinator for Hydrology & Water Resource Management, and I am pleased to welcome you to our chapter. Here, we look at water beyond the classroom, from the systems that sustain life to the challenges that affect our communities and our future. Whether you are already passionate about water or simply curious to learn more, I encourage you to explore, ask questions and get involved. Through field experiences, research, discussion and practical action, let us turn our understanding into something that matters. I look forward to seeing you explore, learn and grow with us.
